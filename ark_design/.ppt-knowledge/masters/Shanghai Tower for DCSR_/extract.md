## 第 8 页

The whole experience includes on-site viewing and video broadcasting which can be used for 2nd time communication.

整个过程包括现场观看与视频记录的方式，用于二次传播。

On-site viewing: we can select a few spots ideal for viewing the light show, e.g. HuangPu River sightseeing boat, restaurants on the bund, skycrapers on LuJiaZui and etc. We can invites dozens of celebrities or KOLs to selected spots to view the opening light show and help with broadcasting the news. The venues are equipped with stereos to play background music working with the light show.

Also we can use social media to spread through a site where the music is played through radio frequency.

现场观看：场地将以多点少量为原则，选择多个适宜观看灯光秀的地点，如：黄浦江游船、外滩某餐厅、陆家嘴某高楼等。每个地点控制人数，可邀请数十位名人或意见领袖前往观看开幕式灯光秀，利于事件传播。场地可配备音响设备播放与灯光同步的音乐，或利用社交媒体传播，使用手机功能播放广播信号的音乐。

Video broadcasting: the light show will be documented in video format and visually connect the lightshow and it’s LED screen at the bottom (where the grand opening message will be displayed). The video can also be used in social media for 2nd time communication and build buzz.

视频记录：整个灯光秀将同时使用视频记录的方式，视觉连接楼身的灯光秀与楼底的广告位（开幕信息呈现）。视频还可用于后续其他渠道的二次传播，扩大影响力。
