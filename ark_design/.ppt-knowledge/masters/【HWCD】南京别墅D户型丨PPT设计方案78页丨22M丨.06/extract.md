## 第 3 页

ART DECO ELEMENTS

Modern British Art Deco

Art Deco was a pastiche of many different styles, sometimes contradictory,  united by a desire to be modern.

From its outset, Art Deco was influenced by the bold geometric forms of  Cubism; the bright colors of Fauvism and of the Ballets Russes; the updated  craftsmanship of the furniture of the eras of Louis Philippe and Louis XVI; and  the exotic styles of China and Japan, India, Persia, ancient Egypt and Maya  art.

It featured rare and expensive materials, such as ebony and ivory, and  exquisite craftsmanship.

The Oxford and Cambridge Musical Club

MUSICAL CLUB . MAYFAIR

The Oxford and Cambridge Musical Club was founded in London in 1899.

OCMC is a friendly and long established London classical music club where  talented amateur instrumentalists and singers come together to perform and  share a high standard of music making.

The club's original purpose was the performance of chamber music but  expanded over the years to include solo instrumental music, vocal music,  orchestral music and opera.

The heyday of the club in its original form was from 1900 to 1940 and many  famous musicians, politicians and artists of the day were members.

Today the club provides music-making activities in central London with  membership open to all. It has links with the University College London  Chamber Club and the Pro Corda Trust .
