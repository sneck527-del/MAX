气温

TEMPERATURE

南京属北亚热带湿润气候，季风明显，四季分明。年平均气温15.5℃；最热月平均温度 28.1℃，最冷月平 均温度 -2.1℃。气候的变率较大，冬季干旱寒冷，夏季炎热多雨。

Nanjing has a north subtropical humid climate, and the climate is influenced a lot by the monsoom. The  average temperature ranges from -2.1 °C in January to 28.1°C in July, while the annual mean is 15.5 °C.  The summers are humid, the winters are dry.

日照

SUNLIGHT

年平均日照数为2182.4小时。冬季日照时短，夏季日照时长。

The annual sunshine averages is about 2182.4 hours. The length of the day varies significantly over the

course of the year. The shortest day is December 21; the longest day is June 20.

风

WIND

南京市冬、夏间的风向转换十分明显。冬季以东北风为主，夏季以东风和东南风为主，春季以东南和东风为 主，秋季以东北风为主。

The wind direction in Nanjing is veriouse from season to season. In winter and autum, the seasonal domain  wind direction is from Northeast. In spring and summer, the seasonal domain wind direction is from East  ans Southeast.

降雨

PRECIPITATION

年平均降水量为1047毫米。夏季高温多雨，冬季寒冷少雨，其降水主要集中于夏半年。

The annual average precipitation is about 1047 millimeters.The summers are long, hot and humid, the  winters are short, cold and dry.
