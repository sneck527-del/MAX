高净值人群的消费需求特征：

精神化，希望更多的回归自然

交际化，消费逐渐成为人们联系的纽带和沟 通的平台

圈层化，圈层是一些兴趣相近的人因为特定　　的环境组成同盟体，

个性化，人们告别了那种单调、统一的模式，开始讲究个性、品味、舒适

### Shenzhen Bay Raffles Hotel will be in a prime location.  Well-designed, solid quality and with unique taste; its 100-year brand of service and tradition docking with more mature lifestyle of the international community; these combination will convey to the consumers the ultimate high-end experience.
