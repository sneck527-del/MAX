### Lacquer Line Carving

A traditional art in Minnan, lacquer line carving is a gem in Chinese lacquer work  culture. It featuring exquisite techniques, vivid and true-to-life images, solemn and  antique style and lively tableau, lacquer line carving is an outstanding example in  traditional Chinese art.

Lacquer thread carving has inherited the traditions of Buddhist sculpture art in  ancient times and is inspired by line carving in the Song and Yuan Dynasties,  in particular by leadpowder dropping painting andclay line  carving.
