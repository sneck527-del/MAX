## 第 2 页

### At Jaya International Design, we approach all projects one challenge at a time.  Each solution is a unique synthesis of many threads, bound with the common philosophy of unique, sensitive experiences for the guests. We weave together the location; the intrinsic identities of place and culture and architecture; amplifying the visions of spaces, textures, materials, and the guest; the guest and his/her experience being the main focus of the design philosophy.

### Nanjing, also known as the Southern Capital, has been a major center of culture, research, and tourism. This city has a prominent place in Chinese history and culture, having served as the capitals of various Chinese dynasties. Nanjing is endowed with rich natural resources, which include more than 40 kinds of minerals and possesses abundant water resources, both from Yangtze River and groundwater. The people of Nanjing indulge in natural hot springs and enjoy the beautiful natural scenery by the lakes in the center of the city.  JID envisions this Fullshare-JinLing property to be a calm and relaxing abode-style destination surrounded by outstanding views of the Brunei Garden and the Buddhist Temple. A harmonious composition of natural materials, motifs, and scale, will be important factors in integrating these buildings into its environment being respectful to the surrounding natural habitat.

### JID is taking a contemporary interior design approach with Chinese sensibility reflected through artwork and accessories. Subtly combined with references to Nanjing’s local produce, history and culture; with traces of brocade, ceramics, and river stone handicrafts, all woven together into a harmonious interior tapestry.

### 在JID ，设计师们将每个项目都视作一次全新的挑战和洗礼。每个独特的方案都是从用户的角度设身处地缜密推敲而来。我们善于把捉哲学中的共通性和独特性，发挥自然的优势和能量，通过将当地的风土人情和建筑特色的充分糅合，让特定的机理和纹样、架构和材料装点空间之美，提升用户的感官愉悦，营造祥和的禅意与宁静。对JID而言，顾客的真实感受一直是我们的设计使命和关注焦点。

### 南京贵为十朝古都，素来是文化、考察和旅游的集中之地，它历经了中国历史的风雨洗礼，承载了卓越而悠远的沧桑印记。南京也以其得天独厚的自然资源名闻遐迩，盛产40多种矿物资源，长江水系和地下水系统让城市水资源丰饶而充沛。城中的人们既可享受天然温泉的滋养，又可饱览玄武湖的江南风韵。JID可以预见，这一丰盛与金陵的合作项目必将成为又一座静美闲适的胜地，不负文莱风情园和天龙寺得天独厚的景观。天然材质和图案以宜人的尺度和格局得以精心布置，与周遭建筑和谐共生，同时展现出营造者对佛性之地的由衷敬意。

### 在艺术品及配饰方面，JID将当代的室内设计手法与中式元素结合得恰到好处。设计师们潜心研究南京当地历史、文化和建筑风貌，并巧妙运用云锦、瓷器和雨花石等当地元素，让往来的住客们感受独特的地域魅力和人文情怀。
