## 第 2 页

The Mandarin Oriental Nanjing sits on the banks of the Qin Huai Moat, overlooking the old city. It is at the heart of a prestige residential development, and whilst catering to business travelers during the weekdays, it is envisaged that the hotel’s state-of-the-art health, leisure and dining facilities will be broadly used by the residents’ community on weekends as their de facto ‘clubhouse’, for the entertainment and engagement of family and friends.

The designers propose to create an elegant contemporary interior, seamlessly blending with the building design, whilst incorporating some feel from the residential development of which the hotel is the centerpiece.  The functioning interior will be a combination of honest and high quality materials, with gently-stylized Chinese touches reflecting the location and surroundings, which will prove to be durable both in terms of finish and style, whilst proving to be inspirational and memorable to international and local guests alike.

### 文华东方酒店是着名住宅发展的核心, 在平日为商务旅客提供服务的同时, 预计酒店最先进的健康、休闲和餐饮设施将被广泛采用。居民社区将会在周末作为他们实际上的 ' 会所 ', 为供家人，朋友的娱乐和参与。

### 设计师希望营造一个与建筑设计无缝融合的优雅现代的室内环境,　同时结合住宅开发项目的一些特色，使酒店成为整个项目的视觉核心。促成整个室内设计风格的是一些有质感且制作精良的材料，配以恰到好处的中国传统元素用于映射本项目的地理位置及环境特色。 这将在以后的岁月中体现出不论风格还是品质上的经久不衰，同时证明对国际旅客和当地访客来说都是鼓舞人心和令人难忘的。
